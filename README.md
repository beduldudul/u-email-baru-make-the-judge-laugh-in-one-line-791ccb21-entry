One funny line, 200 characters or fewer, to make the judge laugh.

> My Roomba escaped through the cat flap, and now it sends me postcards from the neighbor's kitchen.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (98 characters, one line, no trailing newline).
